Historical feedback, paraphrased. Reuse the authored tree models, match the surrounding game world to them and plant some singly and others in small groups. The implementation used the exact three variant exports, two scene-specific layouts, lighter distant trees, and matching foliage and litter. Review revealed a camera surrounded by hanging branches and roots inside playable platform geometry. Repositioning the trees restored the entrance view and cleared elevated surfaces. A negative placement regression reproduced the previous failure. The originating game gate passed 183 unit tests and 147 browser checks. Physical controller operation and human visual preferences remain separate acceptance checks.
