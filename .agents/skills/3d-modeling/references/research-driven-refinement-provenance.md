The originating user requested realistic, reference-quality koi anatomy that could be inspected in an exploded view. Research corrected side labeling and the relative size of the gas chambers. A sampled check exposed jaw, fascia, and tail-spine fit issues and guided a scripted revision. Historical results include 83 browser assertions and a matched GLB rebuild. The biological limits were recorded because no matched specimen scan was available. These are originating task results.
