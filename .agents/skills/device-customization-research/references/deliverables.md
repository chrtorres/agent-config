# Report and handoff contracts

## RESEARCH.md

Lead with the current feasibility conclusion and exact target. Summarize live identity versus candidate image identity, all evaluated approaches, evidence and confidence, known incompatibilities, recovery limitations, contradictory claims and exact missing evidence. Include primary links next to source-derived claims and local provenance for actual tool results. Do not equate failed search with impossibility.

## IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md

Identify the best-supported route or explain why none is established. Rank complete solutions separately from partial alternatives. Give ordered prerequisites, implementation phases, stop/exit criteria, rollback and acceptance tests. Mark future commands unexecuted. If no compatible procedure exists, do not fill missing steps with generic flash/unlock instructions. A conditional plan must state where execution currently stops.

## ANALYSIS.md

Retain detailed non-clean acquisition and tool evidence, commands, outputs/digests/paths, internal leads, actual tests, failed attempts, coverage and limitations. Preserve historical results but place current status/corrections prominently. Record concise evidence-based rationales and decision summaries, not private internal reasoning.

## SPEC.md

Independent contract only: externally supported capabilities, behavior, interfaces, I/O, state transitions, functional requirements and acceptance tests. Label live facts, manufacturer claims, owner reports, proposed requirements and unknowns. Do not import internal paths, decompiler output, proprietary code/algorithms or untested driver assumptions. Preserve unknown timing/media/protocol choices rather than invent values. Include restart, privacy, cancellation and failure behavior when relevant to the objective.

## Completion and separation

Research is complete once every major applicable branch is answered, scoped-incompatible or precisely unresolved, and the recommended route or absence of one is supported. Do not conflate research completion, feasibility, installation readiness and acceptance-test success.

Only reviewed clean contracts enter reconstruction. Firmware, databases, raw transcripts, implementation-derived reports/plans and analysis scripts stay non-clean. Separate workspace access must actually be provisioned by the owner; writing a statement about isolation does not enforce it. Hash manifests prove recorded content consistency, not authenticity or completeness.
